About

A calm notebook on your computer

Lanruj is a bullet journal app for Linux. It keeps the method as it is on paper: short lines for tasks, events and notes, a page for each day, week and month, and a moment every evening to decide what is still worth doing.

Why Lanruj

Many note and to-do apps want an account, a subscription or a place in the cloud. Lanruj wants none of that. It is made for people who like the bullet journal method and want it on their computer too, without giving up their notes to anyone.

What it believes in

  • Your notes are plain Markdown files in a folder on your computer.
  • It works offline, always, with no account and no ads.
  • Encryption is there when you want it, with a recovery key.
  • It is free software: anyone can read, change and share the code.

How it is made

Lanruj is written in Rust, with GTK 4 and libadwaita, so it fits naturally on the GNOME desktop. It speaks English and Romanian and comes with seven fonts for your pages, including two that look like handwriting.

Who makes it

Lanruj is made by ThinkRoot, an independent developer. Ideas, bug reports and kind words are always welcome on the contact page.
