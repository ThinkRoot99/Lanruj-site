Privacy

Privacy policy

In short: Lanruj does not collect anything about you. Your journal stays on your computer, and this website does not track you.

Last updated: 27 November 2026

The app

  • No data leaves your computer. Lanruj has no account, no cloud, no statistics and no telemetry. It does not connect to the internet on its own; it only opens a link in your browser when you click one.
  • Your journal is a folder of files (~/.lanruj) on your computer. Backups and exported archives go only where you choose.
  • Encryption is optional. When it is on, your files are encrypted on your computer with your password. Nobody else, including the author of Lanruj, can read them or recover the password.
  • The keyring is optional. If you ask Lanruj to remember the password, it is kept in your system's keyring, on your computer.
  • Syncing is your choice. If you sync the journal between computers, for example with Syncthing, the files travel between your own devices, as you set it up.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no ads. The site loads no fonts, scripts or images from other websites.
  • Your language choice and, if you use the button, your choice of light or dark mode are kept in your browser's local storage, on your device, only so the site opens the way you left it. You can clear them from your browser at any time.
  • Server logs. Like most websites, the server that hosts this site may keep standard access logs (such as the address of your device and the time of the visit) for security and to keep the site running. They are not used to identify or follow you.
